03 / Kitchen notes

Little lessons for
everyday nourishment.

Practical guidance from the NourishNest playbook: plan with your real schedule, shop with intention, store food safely, and build balanced plates without making meals feel restrictive.

01
↗

Build a menu that flexes

Use the simple 3–2–1 rhythm: three vegetables, two proteins, and one flexible comfort meal to keep the week balanced.

02
◌

Shop, store, stretch

Choose fresh ingredients, compare prices, store raw food safely, and reuse ingredients before they become waste.

03
⌁

Make room for enough

Think in portions and patterns: include protein, fiber-rich sides, water, and enough food to support your actual day.

04
⌂

Keep food safe

Separate raw and cooked ingredients, refrigerate promptly, and use temperature and storage cues to protect the people you feed.
